# Code of Conduct

Knowledge follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Summary

- Treat everyone with respect. Assume good intent.
- Technical disagreement is fine; personal attacks are not.
- Harassment of any kind is prohibited.
- The maintainers' goal is a productive, focused engineering community. Behavior that undermines that goal will be addressed.

## Enforcement

Report issues privately to `legal@fulminate.io`. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers and handled confidentially. The maintainers may warn, temporarily suspend, or permanently remove contributors whose behavior violates this code.

For the complete text, including the full list of expected and unacceptable behaviors and enforcement guidelines, see the canonical [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
